# CTA Strategy: Get a Demo vs Start Free Trial

## Current setup (authority, no desperation)

- **Hero CTA:** Get a Demo  
- **Nav (desktop):** Pricing | Plans | Features | Contact | Sign in (text) | **Get a Demo**  
- **Nav (mobile):** Hamburger only. Menu: Features, Pricing, How it works, Contact, **Sign in** (text link only, not a button). **Get a Demo** = sticky bottom button (always visible).  
- **Pricing CTA:** Book a Demo  
- **No Free Trial** anywhere.

**Why this works:** Clear authority. One primary ask. Sign in is for existing users only (text, never a button). Mobile users get one persistent CTA.

---

## Correct order of evolution

1. **Get a Demo** (now) — primary everywhere.  
2. **Request Access** (later) — if you add a waitlist or gated access before full trial.  
3. **Start Free Trial** (only when ready) — see below.

---

## Do NOT add “Start Free Trial” yet if

- You still need onboarding help (calls/setup).
- Value isn’t obvious in &lt;10 minutes.
- You sell to owners who want reassurance.
- The product spans many workflows (JANIBEAR does).

**That’s JANIBEAR right now — stay demo-led.**

---

## When to add “Start Free Trial” (later)

Add **“Start Free Trial”** only when:

- Demo → “Try it yourself” conversion is proven.
- You have guided onboarding.
- The product has a strong “aha” moment fast.
- You want velocity over control.

---

## Future CTA structure (6–12 months out)

When the market already trusts you and self-serve is ready:

- **Primary:** Start Free Trial  
- **Secondary:** Book a Demo  

That flip only happens after the above conditions are met.
