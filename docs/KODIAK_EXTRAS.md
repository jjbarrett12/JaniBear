# Kodiak Extras — Sales + Ops + What’s Included Only in Kodiak

**Tier framing:**
- **Cub** = Sales only  
- **Grizzly** = Sales + Ops  
- **Kodiak** = Sales + Ops + **extras** (below)

Use this list for pricing pages, sales conversations, and in-app upgrade prompts.

---

## 1. Scale & limits

| Extra | One-liner for marketing |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **Unlimited users** | No seat cap—add your whole team. |
| **Unlimited locations** | Every site in one place (Cub/Grizzly have caps). |
| **Multi-region / multi-entity reporting** | Roll up by region, brand, or entity (especially for franchisors). |

*Use:* “Kodiak removes the caps. Grow without hitting limits.”

---

## 2. Visibility & reporting

| Extra | One-liner for marketing |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **Executive dashboards** | High-level KPIs and trends for leadership. |
| **KMI / advanced KPI dashboards** | Key Metric Index, outcome rankings, weighted scores (franchisor outcome review). |
| **Cross-region / cross-location analytics** | Compare performance across territories or franchisees. |
| **Audit trail & export** | Who did what, when—for compliance and reviews. |

*Use:* “See the whole picture. Executive views and audit-ready history.”

---

## 3. Training & enablement

| Extra | One-liner for marketing |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **Jani-Bear University** | Full training library (also in Grizzly; call out “full access” in Kodiak). |
| **Dedicated or extended onboarding** | Setup and training tailored to your org (optional; “included” or “discounted”). |
| **Custom training playlists** (future) | Curate courses by role or franchisee (Kodiak-only if you add it). |

*Use:* “Train everyone the same way. Optional white-glove onboarding.”

---

## 4. Operations & tools

| Extra | One-liner for marketing |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **HelpHubQR included** | QR issue reporting and ops tasks—no per-location fee (Cub & Grizzly: $29/location). |
| **SLA & compliance tracking** | Track response times and outcome compliance (no labor control; franchisor-friendly). |
| **Brand compliance / suggested standards visibility** | See adoption of suggested standards (outcome review only). |

*Use:* “HelpHubQR included. Compliance and standards visibility without extra fees.”

---

## 5. Support & success

| Extra | One-liner for marketing |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **Priority support** | Faster response SLA (e.g. next-business-day or 4-hour for critical). |
| **Dedicated success contact** (optional) | Single point of contact for questions and best practices. |
| **Quarterly business review** (optional) | Short call to review usage, KPIs, and next steps. |

*Use:* “Priority support and optional dedicated success.”

---

## 6. Integrations & automation (future)

| Extra | One-liner for marketing |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **API access** | Integrate with ERP, payroll, or other tools. |
| **Advanced AI** | Deal prioritization, forecast signals, or pipeline health (when you ship it). |

*Use:* “Connect your stack. API and advanced AI when you need them.”

---

## Suggested Kodiak tagline

**“Everything in Grizzly, plus unlimited scale, executive visibility, HelpHubQR included, and priority support.”**

Or shorter:

**“Sales + Ops + scale, visibility, and priority support.”**

---

## Checklist for pricing UI

When you list Kodiak on the pricing page or in the survey, you can show:

- [ ] Unlimited users & locations  
- [ ] Executive & KMI / KPI dashboards  
- [ ] Cross-region / multi-entity reporting  
- [ ] HelpHubQR included (no per-location fee)  
- [ ] Audit trail & export  
- [ ] Priority support  
- [ ] Jani-Bear University (full access)  
- [ ] Dedicated onboarding (optional or included)  
- [ ] API access (if/when available)  

Adjust the list per business model (owner-operator vs franchisor vs franchisee) so franchisors see “KMI / outcome dashboards” and “suggested standards visibility,” and operators see “unlimited locations” and “HelpHubQR included.”
