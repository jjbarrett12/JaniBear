# JaniBear Pricing Strategy & Value Justification

## Positioning

**One platform does the work of sales + QC/ops—so you cut headcount without cutting quality.**

Pricing is anchored to **headcount replacement**, not “per-seat software.” Each tier answers: *“What role(s) does this replace, and what would that cost in salary?”*

---

## Pricing model: per company (not per seat)

**Recommendation: price per company, with included users per tier.**

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **Per company (flat)** | Simple to sell; matches "replace headcount" story; no seat shock when adding someone; SMB-friendly. | Revenue doesn't scale with team size. |
| **Per seat** | Revenue scales with usage; familiar in SaaS. | Can feel like nickel-and-diming; complicates "replace a hire" message. |

**Why per company fits JaniBear:** You're selling an *outcome* (one rep's output, or a team's throughput), not "software per person." The tier already defines scope (1 user for Cub, team for Black Bear, etc.), so the price is for the *company* and the tier includes a defined number of users. No "per seat" line item to explain.

**Included users (suggested):**

- **Cub:** 1 user (single rep).
- **Black Bear:** Up to 5 users (team).
- **Grizzly:** Up to 10 users (sales + ops).
- **Kodiak:** Unlimited or custom.

If you later need to scale revenue with very large teams, you can add an "additional users" overage (e.g. Black Bear + $X per user over 5) without changing the main message.

## Tier Architecture (Sales + QC Unified)

### 🐾 Cub — *“I need someone selling now—without hiring.”*

| | |
|--|--|
| **Value** | Replaces **1 sales rep** (first hire). One rep’s output, zero HR. |
| **Headcount anchor** | Junior SDR / inside sales: ~$45–65k/year + benefits. |
| **Suggested price** | **$59/mo** ($708/year) |
| **Justification** | ~1–1.5% of the cost of one FTE. “Okay… this actually replaces my first hire.” |

**Sales:** AI copilot, prebuilt sequences, lead intake & qualification, call/meeting summaries, proposal & quote help, CRM sync.  
**QC/Ops (light):** Activity tracking, basic deal timeline, auto-logged actions, minimal analytics.  
**Limits:** 1 pipeline, 1 motion, 1 user.

---

### 🐻 Black Bear — *“We have leads. We need throughput.”*

| | |
|--|--|
| **Value** | Replaces **sales at scale**—team-level output without new hires. |
| **Headcount anchor** | 2–3 SDRs/AEs: ~$120–180k/year. |
| **Suggested price** | **$149/mo** ($1,788/year) |
| **Justification** | ~1% of avoided team cost. “This is a sales team without salaries.” |

**Everything in Cub, plus:** Multi-pipeline, parallel sequences, lead routing, territory/segment logic, shared inbox & task pool, AI objection handling.  
**QC/Ops (sales-focused):** Rep benchmarking, pipeline health, follow-up SLA, stalled-deal detection, conversion analytics.  
**Limits:** 2–5 pipelines, multiple motions, team dashboards.

---

### 🐻 Grizzly — *“Sales is working—now keep it consistent.”*

| | |
|--|--|
| **Value** | Replaces **sales ops + cleans up the mess sales creates.** QC becomes visible and valuable. |
| **Headcount anchor** | Sales ops / rev ops role: ~$60–100k/year. |
| **Suggested price** | **$249/mo** ($2,988/year) |
| **Justification** | Replaces a fraction of one ops FTE; prevents revenue leakage and compliance risk. “Sales ops and consistency, without the hire.” |

**Everything in Black Bear, plus:** Enforced playbooks, deal QA before stage movement, required artifacts per stage, AI deal audits, handoff workflows, custom QC rules, exception alerts, audit trail, ops dashboard.  
**Limits:** Sales + Ops roles, custom workflows, configurable QC.

---

### 🐻 Kodiak — *“I want predictable revenue without babysitting.”*

| | |
|--|--|
| **Value** | **Full revenue engine**—headcount annihilation. Revenue department, not just software. |
| **Headcount anchor** | Multi-team (sales + ops + success), forecast/strategy: $200k+ in equivalent headcount. |
| **Suggested price** | **$499/mo** or **Contact us** (custom/enterprise) |
| **Justification** | Replaces coordination, forecasting, and cross-team ops. “Revenue department in a box.” |

**Everything in Grizzly, plus:** AI deal prioritization, forecast confidence, pipeline rebalancing, multi-team orchestration, continuous deal monitoring, revenue leakage detection, SLA auto-remediation, executive dashboards, multi-org/API, dedicated onboarding, priority support/SLA.

## Price Summary (Suggested) — per company

| Tier | Price/mo | Price/year | Included users | Replaces (rough equivalent) |
|------|----------|------------|----------------|------------------------------|
| Cub | $59 | $708 | 1 | 1 sales rep |
| Black Bear | $149 | $1,788 | Up to 5 | Sales team (2–3 reps) |
| Grizzly | $249 | $2,988 | Up to 10 | Sales + sales ops |
| Kodiak | $499+ | Custom | Unlimited / custom | Full revenue org |

All prices are **per company**, not per seat. Each tier includes the stated number of users.

- **Black Bear:** “Sales at scale, no new hires. **A sales team without salaries.**”
- **Grizzly:** “Sales + ops under control. **Replaces sales ops and cleans up the mess.**”
- **Kodiak:** “Full revenue engine. **A revenue department, not software.**”

## Alternative Pricing (if you want to test)

- **More aggressive entry:** Cub $49/mo (strong “under $50” hook).  
- **Wider spread:** Cub $59 → Black Bear $179 → Grizzly $299 → Kodiak “Contact us” only.  
- **Annual discount:** Offer ~2 months free on annual (e.g. $59/mo → $590/year) to improve LTV and reduce churn.
