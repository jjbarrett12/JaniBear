# Hero Video: Commercial Cleaning Crews in Large Accounts

Use this for the homepage hero video. **I can’t generate video files**—use the script below to commission a clip or to search for stock footage.

---

## How to add your video to the header

1. **Add the video file** to the `public` folder:
   - **File name:** `hero-video.mp4` (or we can point to `.webm` if you prefer)
   - **Optional poster (thumbnail before play):** `hero-video-poster.jpg` in `public/`

2. **Recommended specs** (for fast load and good quality):
   - **Length:** 15–30 seconds (loops in the hero)
   - **Resolution:** 1920×1080 (1080p) is enough
   - **Format:** MP4 (H.264); keep file under ~5–8 MB for the hero
   - **No sound needed** — the hero video is muted and autoplays

3. **If you use a hosted URL instead** (e.g. Vimeo/YouTube embed or CDN), we can switch the hero to an `<iframe>` or `<video src="https://...">`; say the URL and I’ll wire it up.

## Short script (30–45 seconds): “Commercial cleaning crews in large accounts”

**Concept:** Show professional cleaning crews working inside a large commercial building (office, hospital, school, or retail). Clean, calm, competent—the kind of accounts JANIBEAR serves.

**Suggested shots (pick 4–6):**

| Time   | Shot description |
|--------|-------------------|
| 0–5s   | Wide: Empty corridor or lobby, early morning or evening. One or two crew members enter or walk through with cart/supplies. |
| 5–12s  | Medium: Crew member cleaning glass or wiping surfaces in a lobby or office. Focus on methodical, professional motion. |
| 12–20s | Close-up: Spray bottle, mop, or cart—hands at work. No faces required if you want to keep it generic. |
| 20–28s | Medium: Another crew member in a different area (restroom, break room, or hallway). Same tone—calm, systematic. |
| 28–35s | Wide: Same or different large space, crew in background. Suggests “whole building” and scale. |
| 35–45s | Optional: Crew leaving or clocking out; or a quick cut of a supervisor with tablet/phone (suggests “operations”). |

**Tone:** Professional, quiet, slightly cinematic. Natural or soft lighting. No dialogue. Music (if any) should be minimal and neutral.

**Use this script when:**
- Briefing a videographer
- Searching stock sites (search terms below)
- Ordering a custom clip from a video producer

## Where to get the video (stock footage)

I can’t create the video for you; use one of these approaches:

### 1. Free stock video (royalty-free)

- **Pexels Videos** — https://www.pexels.com/videos/  
  Search: `cleaning crew`, `janitor commercial`, `office cleaning`, `cleaning staff`, `building cleaning`, `commercial cleaning`, `cleaning corridor`, `cleaning lobby`

- **Pixabay Videos** — https://pixabay.com/videos/  
  Same search terms as above.

- **Coverr** — https://coverr.co  
  Search: `cleaning`, `office`, `janitor`, `building`.

- **Videvo** — https://www.videvo.net  
  Filter for free and “no attribution” if possible; search `cleaning`, `janitor`, `office cleaning`.

### 2. Paid stock (often higher quality)

- **Artgrid / Storyblocks** — `commercial cleaning`, `janitor`, `office cleaning`, `cleaning crew`
- **Shutterstock / Getty** — same terms; good for “large building” and “cleaning staff”
- **Envato Elements** — search “cleaning” or “janitorial” for B-roll

### 3. Custom shoot

Use the **Short script** section above as a one-pager for a local videographer. One morning or evening in a real office or school often gives the best “large account” look.
